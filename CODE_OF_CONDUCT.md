# Code of Conduct

Bug Hunter is a technical project. Keep discussion focused on making the skill more accurate and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence, not people.
- Back technical claims with examples, sources, or reproductions where you can.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that do not fit the project's scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Posting secrets, credentials, or malicious code.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break this code, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, follow the private-contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mark your report as a conduct issue.
